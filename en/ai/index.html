Your own AI in Zélar

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Zélar is built so your own AI can work with your cellar. Besides the prompts and paste-import under Prompts, you can scan labels with a Shortcut, share replies straight from your AI app, and let your AI read in a whole cellar the first time. It's free, and it will stay free.

Whichever way you use, one rule holds: everything that comes from an AI is a suggestion. It lands as a draft that you check before anything goes into your stock or the Journal.

Three ways in

Pick the one that fits the job

  • Scan a label with a Shortcut. Take a photo; the Shortcut reads the text on your phone, asks your own Claude or ChatGPT, and puts the reply and the photo into Zélar as a draft. Best for one bottle at a time.
  • Share from your AI app. Already talked to your AI about a wine? Share the reply (and the photo, if you like) to Zélar from the share sheet.
  • First load. A whole cellar from a spreadsheet, another app's export or a pile of label photos — as text in parts, or as a backup file.

Privacy

What goes where

  • The photo stays on your phone when the Shortcut reads the label with iOS's text recognition. If you choose Apple's cloud model to read the text instead, the photo is processed in Apple's Private Cloud Compute.
  • Only text goes to your own Claude or ChatGPT, under your own account. It counts toward your plan's limits there, and that service's terms apply.
  • For a first load, you send your spreadsheet or photos to your own AI account yourself.
  • Zélar sends nothing. The Shortcuts actions and the share sheet only read and write files on your device. Drafts stay on the device and aren't synced.

The Shortcut

Install "Zélar · Scan label"

There are two versions of the Shortcut, one for Claude and one for ChatGPT. Pick the one that matches the AI app you use.

Links coming

The Shortcuts "Zélar · Skan etiket (Claude)" and "Zélar · Skan etiket (ChatGPT)" will be linked here from iCloud once they're ready. Until then you can build it yourself from the recipe below; it takes a few minutes.

What you need

  • Zélar, and the Claude or ChatGPT app, installed and signed in.
  • The Shortcuts app (comes with iOS).
  • Optional: Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or later) to let Apple's model read the text. It has daily limits. Without it, the Shortcut uses iOS's built-in text recognition, which works fine on most labels.

The first time you run it

iOS asks whether the Shortcut may use Zélar, your AI app and Photos. It's the Shortcut asking, not Zélar, and the answer only applies to this Shortcut. Choose Always Allow so it doesn't ask every time.

Put it where you'll use it

  • The Action button (iPhone 15 Pro and later): Settings → Action Button → Shortcut.
  • The Lock Screen or Control Center: add a Shortcut control.
  • The Home Screen: touch and hold the Shortcut in Shortcuts → Share → Add to Home Screen.
  • The share sheet in Photos: turn on "Show in Share Sheet" in the Shortcut's details, and you can scan a photo you've already taken.

Build it yourself

Create a new Shortcut in Shortcuts with these actions. Zélar's actions are under Apps → Zélar.

1  Receive Images from Share Sheet — if there's no input: Take Photo
2  Extract Text from Image (Shortcut Input)
     or: Use Model (Cloud) "Transcribe all visible text."
3  Choose from Menu: Stock · Journal
4  (Journal) Ask for Input: "Rating, place, glass or bottle?"
5  Zélar → Get Zélar Prompt
     Prompt: Scan label to stock / Scan label to the journal
     Note: (the answer from step 4)
6  Ask Claude: [Zélar Prompt] [Extracted Text]
     (ChatGPT version: Ask ChatGPT, text only)
7  Zélar → Create Stocked Wine / Create Journal Entry
     Text: [Response]   Photos: [Shortcut Input]

The prompt in step 5 is updated along with Zélar, so the Shortcut doesn't need rebuilding if the format changes.

Scan a label

Step by step

  1. Run the Shortcut and photograph the label, straight on and in good light.
  2. Choose Stock or Journal. Stock is for a bottle going into the cellar. Journal is for a wine you're tasting now; write a short note with rating, place and glass or bottle, for example "4.5 stars, Restaurant Barolo, a glass".
  3. Wait for your AI. The Shortcut sends the label's text and Zélar's prompt to Claude or ChatGPT and waits for the reply.
  4. Zélar opens the draft with the reply and the photo, ready to check.

The review screen

  • The fields are AI suggestions: check name, producer and vintage against the label and correct what's wrong. AI can be wrong.
  • If the wine is already in your cellar, Zélar suggests using it, so the bottle is added to the existing wine instead of becoming a new one.
  • Choose how many bottles and where they go. Without a place they land under No location.
  • Tap Finish up. Only now is the wine or tasting created, and the draft deleted.
Share from your AI app

The reply from Claude or ChatGPT straight to Zélar

  1. Ask your AI to reply in Zélar's format. The easiest way is to start with a prompt from Prompts or the format from ＋ → Import from AI → ?.
  2. Touch and hold the reply (or use the share button) → Share → Zélar.
  3. The "Share to Zélar" card shows what the text holds, for example a wine's name or "3 wines in the text", and whether it looks like an AI reply in Zélar's format.
  4. Choose Wine to stock or Tasted wine. Zélar preselects the one that fits best. You check it in Zélar before anything is created.

Text and photo separately

Many AI apps share text only. Share the photo afterwards (or first) from Photos to Zélar, and choose Add to … with the draft it belongs to. Zélar suggests the draft that fits but never picks one by itself. If you share a photo on its own, you can also Save as draft and wait for the text, or go straight to the wine or tasting form with the pictures.

Can't find Zélar in the share sheet? Scroll to the end of the row of apps, tap More and turn Zélar on.

Drafts

What's waiting for you

While there are drafts, Drafts shows with a count in the menu (behind the cellar button on Overview) under Library, and in the iPad sidebar. A draft can be:

StateWhat to do
Text and photo · ready to checkTap it to check and create.
No photo · ready to checkCreate it as it is, or share a photo to it first.
Waiting for the AI's textShare the reply from your AI app and choose Add to draft, or create the wine yourself now.
  • Delete a draft with Delete draft, or tidy up with Delete drafts older than 30 days.
  • The paste button in Drafts saves a copied AI reply as a draft. Use it with apps that can only copy.
  • Drafts stay on the device. They aren't synced to your other devices and aren't included in backups.
First load

The whole cellar in one go

Already have your wines recorded somewhere else? Find First load under Backup, Import & Export in the menu. There are two routes.

As text (recommended)

  1. Tap Copy AI instructions (text).
  2. Open Claude or ChatGPT, paste the instructions and attach your spreadsheet, export or photos.
  3. The AI answers in parts of 40 wines. Write continue to get the next part.
  4. Copy each part and paste it under Paste wines from AI in the same section, one at a time. Check the wines and tap Finish up.
  5. Add photos to each wine afterwards.

As a backup file — at your own risk

Read this first

A backup file from an AI is restored with Restore from file, and that replaces everything in Zélar. The AI can make mistakes in the file, and Zélar can't merge it into an existing cellar (yet). Only use it in an empty app, and make a backup first if you have data.

  1. Tap Copy AI instructions (backup file) and confirm with I understand, copy.
  2. Use an AI that can create files. Paste the instructions and attach your files; photos can be included.
  3. Download the .zelarbackup file the AI makes to Files.
  4. In Zélar: Backup, Import & Export → Restore from file → pick the file in Files.

If the file gets too large, the AI splits it into several. Restore only one of them, since each restore replaces the last, or use the text route instead.

Tips

  • Spreadsheets: one row per wine with headers gives the best result. Bottle count and location can each have a column.
  • Another app's export: CSV or text works best. Tell the AI which app it comes from.
  • Many labels: one photo per label, straight on, sent in smaller batches.
  • Locations: build your racks in Zélar first. Then the AI can use your real names, for example "Rack A · B3".
Troubleshooting

When something's off

"The reply uses a newer version of the format"

The reply was written for a newer Zélar. Update Zélar in the App Store and try again.

The Shortcut stops, or Ask Claude / Ask ChatGPT gives no reply

Check that the AI app is installed and signed in, and that you're online. Open the AI app once, then run the Shortcut from the Shortcuts app so you can see which step stops.

The label was misread

Retake the photo straight on, without glare, with the label filling the picture. If text recognition struggles, try Apple's cloud model in step 2. You can always correct the fields on the review screen.

Zélar isn't in the share sheet

Scroll to the end of the row of apps, tap More and turn Zélar on. Open Zélar once after a new install.

"Use Model" stops

Apple's model has daily limits. Use text recognition in step 2 until tomorrow.

Why not just Siri?

Siri's new AI features aren't available on iPhone in the EU yet. Shortcuts work everywhere, so Zélar builds on them.

The file is too large

Split the job: send fewer photos at a time, or use the text route with parts of 40 wines.

For the curious

For your own prompts and Shortcuts

The format

Zélar reads text with one detail per line (KEY: value) and a --- line between wines. A reply can start with ZÉLAR-FORMAT: 1, so Zélar knows which version of the format it is. Danish and English keys both work, and lines Zélar doesn't know are skipped.

ZÉLAR-FORMAT: 1
WINE: Château Somewhere
PRODUCER: A. Christmann
VINTAGE: 2018
COUNTRY: France
REGION: Bordeaux, Médoc
TYPE: Red
GRAPES: Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot
QUANTITY: 2
DRINKING WINDOW: 2024-2032
CELLAR: Home
LOCATION: Rack A · B3
---
WINE: ...

Get the full format, with every field, from Get Import Format in Shortcuts or with ? under ＋ → Import from AI.

Your prompt library in a Shortcut

Get Prompt With My Cellar followed by Ask Claude or Ask ChatGPT gives you your prompts without opening Zélar. Two ideas:

  • Food pairing by voice: Dictate Text ("What are you cooking?") → Get Prompt With My Cellar (Food pairing for dinner, Dish: the dictated text) → Ask Claude.
  • Shopping suggestions on the Home Screen: Get Prompt With My Cellar (Shopping suggestions) → Ask ChatGPT.

The reply stays in the AI app. If you use the task Enrich my wine details, paste the reply with Update wines from AI in Zélar, so you can see the changes field by field.
