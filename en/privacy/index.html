Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Zélar is a personal wine cellar app, built and maintained by one independent developer as a small hobby project. This policy explains what data Zélar collects, where it is stored, and the choices you have.

In short: your cellar data stays on your device and, if you want, in your own iCloud. Besides that, two kinds of data leave your device: anonymized usage statistics, which you can turn off, and, only if you choose to use the AI features, the photo or text you send with that one request.

Your wine cellar data

Stored locally on your device

Everything you enter in Zélar — cellars, racks, shelves, wines, bottles, tasting notes, drinking windows, photos, wish list and drafts — is stored locally on your iPhone or iPad. It is not sent to the developer's server, and no one but you has access to it.

Optional iCloud sync

Zélar can sync your cellar between your devices through your personal iCloud account (Apple CloudKit). Your data is then stored in your own private iCloud container, under Apple's privacy practices.

  • You can turn sync off and on in the app's Settings.
  • The developer has no access to data in your iCloud — it is tied to your account in the same way as your Photos or Notes.
  • Drafts (AI replies and photos waiting for you to check them) stay on the device and are not synced.

Your own AI: clipboard, sharing and Shortcuts

Zélar can give you parts of your cellar as text (prompts and Markdown), so you can use them with an AI assistant of your choice, and can read structured text you paste back. This happens through the clipboard, the share sheet ("Share to Zélar") and Zélar's actions in Apple's Shortcuts app. None of these routes use the network: they only read and write on your device. You move the text to your AI, and what you send there is between you and that service, under your own account and its terms.

Zélar AI

AI features (label scan and more)

Zélar AI is part of Zélar Pro and is optional. The features are off until you agree on the consent screen the first time you use one. You can withdraw consent at any time under Settings → AI features, and they stop immediately.

What is sent

  • Scan label: the photo you took, reduced in size (at most 1568 pixels on the long side). Our server adds a fixed instruction and sends both to Anthropic, which reads the label and returns the wine's details.
  • Enrich online: text only — the name, producer, vintage, country, region, appellation and grapes of one wine. You get suggestions back, which you review before anything is saved. The reply may include an estimated market value.
  • Dish suggestions in reminders (optional, turned on separately): the same fields plus the wine type, for a wine past its drinking window, at most once per wine.

Nothing else from your cellar is sent — no notes, prices, ratings, tags or other wines. Our server rejects requests with other fields.

Device identifier

To stop abuse and to keep track of your use of Zélar AI, the app proves to our server that it is a genuine copy of Zélar using Apple's App Attest. This creates a key in your device's Secure Enclave; our server stores the key's identifier, its public key and a counter. It is not your Apple ID, it is not shared with anyone, and it changes if you reinstall the app.

Proof of purchase (Zélar Pro)

If you buy Zélar Pro, the app also sends Apple's signed record of that purchase with each AI request, so our server can give you Zélar AI as part of Pro. The record is created and signed by Apple. It contains the app's bundle ID, which Pro product you bought (monthly, yearly or lifetime), when you bought it, when a subscription expires or was refunded, whether it was bought in the App Store or Apple's test environment, and Apple's transaction identifiers. It does not contain your name, email address, Apple ID, payment method or price.

Our server checks Apple's signature and keeps only the original transaction identifier, which stays the same for this purchase on all your devices and after a reinstall, so Zélar AI follows your purchase. If a renewal payment fails and Apple gives you a grace period to fix it, the app also sends Apple's signed renewal status, so you keep Zélar AI until then; our server only checks it and stores nothing from it. None of this is sent before you have agreed to the AI features, and it is never used for advertising or shared with anyone.

Fair use

With a subscription, Zélar AI is included under fair use: up to 60 AI actions a month and 30 a day. To keep track of this, our server counts your actions per device identifier or per purchase. The count contains nothing about your wines.

Who processes it

  • Cloudflare, Inc. runs our server. Requests pass through Cloudflare's network.
  • Anthropic, PBC runs the AI model. Anthropic processes the photo or text to answer the request and does not use it to train its models. Anthropic deletes requests and answers automatically after a short period stated in its Privacy Center, and keeps them longer only if needed to enforce its Usage Policy or to comply with the law.

Both act as our processors under data processing agreements, and processing takes place in the United States. Transfers rely, for Anthropic, on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses in its data processing addendum, with the UK and Swiss addenda, and for Cloudflare, on its certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, with the EU Standard Contractual Clauses as a fallback.

What our server keeps

No photos and no text. Our server keeps only the App Attest identifier, public key and counter, your usage count, the original transaction identifier if you bought Pro, and operational logs. The logs contain the request type, result code, plan, number of actions, token counts, estimated cost and duration — never the photo, the text, the AI's answer or your IP address. Operational logs are deleted automatically after at most 7 days.

AI can be wrong

Answers from an AI can be wrong, including facts and prices. Check them before you save. Zélar saves nothing from a scan or an enrichment until you have reviewed it and tapped save.

Legal basis

Your consent (GDPR art. 6(1)(a)). Withdrawing it does not affect requests already made.

Your data stays yours

The result of a scan is saved only on your device and in your iCloud, like everything else you enter.

From our server

Settings and exchange rates

Zélar fetches a small settings file from our server, for example how many bottles the free plan includes, and any notes from the developer. Once you use a currency other than your own, it also fetches exchange rates: the European Central Bank's euro reference rates, which our server collects from the ECB and passes on unchanged.

Neither request contains an identifier or anything about you or your cellar — not even which currencies you use — and everyone gets the same answer. Cloudflare sees your IP address to deliver the answer; we do not log it. These requests don't need consent to the AI features.

Share a cellar

Sharing a cellar (beta)

You can share a cellar with up to four other people. Sharing is optional and off until you turn it on under Settings → Experimental.

  • How it works. Sharing uses Apple's iCloud: everyone uses their own Apple ID, and Zélar has no account and no copy of your data. A shared cellar lives in the owner's iCloud; the people invited read and change it through their own iCloud. Apple shows each person's name or email address to the others, as with any iCloud share.
  • What the others see. The shared cellar's racks, bottles, who drank them, tags and wine photos. If the owner has Zélar Pro, the cellar also holds a proof of that purchase from the App Store, so the others' apps can tell the cellar is Pro. It stays in iCloud and is never sent to our server.
  • What stays private. Your tasting notes, ratings and occasions, the Journal, your wish list, your label scans and your other cellars are never shared.
  • Leaving. You can leave a shared cellar, and the owner can remove people or stop sharing, at any time. Your own tasting notes stay with you.
Usage statistics

PostHog

Zélar uses PostHog, a product analytics service, to understand how the app is used and to find bugs and room for improvement. Analytics data is processed on PostHog's servers in the EU.

The statistics are limited to anonymized events about using the app, such as which screens are opened, which features are used, and counts or codes. They never contain your wines, notes, prices or other personal information, and Zélar is designed so they can't identify you.

  • You can opt out at any time under Settings → Privacy. After that, no more usage data is sent.
  • The statistics are kept apart from your cellar data and from Zélar AI.

What Zélar doesn't do
  • No accounts, sign-ups or passwords.
  • No ads and no third-party ad networks.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with third parties for marketing.
  • No AI feature sends anything before you have agreed, and none sends your whole cellar.
Children's privacy

Zélar is an app for managing wine cellars and is intended for adults of legal drinking age in their country. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to access, correct, delete or export your data.

Because your cellar data is stored on your device, you have direct control over it: you can export, edit or delete it in the app, or remove it entirely by deleting the app.

To withdraw consent for the AI features, turn them off under Settings → AI features. To have your App Attest identifier and usage count deleted from our server, write to anders@whaaat.co with the date you first used the AI features. Because we hold no account or name, we may ask you to reinstall the app instead, which creates a new identifier. For questions about PostHog data, write to the same address or opt out in the app.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the app or in the law. The "Last updated" date at the top always shows the latest revision. Continued use of Zélar after changes take effect counts as acceptance of the updated policy.
