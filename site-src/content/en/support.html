Support & User Guide

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Zélar is a personal wine cellar for your iPhone and iPad. You build your racks and wine fridges as they stand at home, put each bottle where it actually lies, and let the app keep track of what's ready to drink. The name comes from the Portuguese zelar — to watch over, to take care of.

Zélar is a small, independent hobby project. Everything is stored on your device (with optional iCloud sync), and there's no account to create. Zélar is built so that your own AI can work with your data: ready-made prompts, importing AI replies and exports are free and work offline.

This guide walks through the whole app. AI Shortcuts, sharing from your AI app and the first load of a whole cellar have a guide of their own: Your own AI in Zélar. If you have a question the guide doesn't answer, write to zelar@whaaat.co.

Find your way around

Four tabs, one ＋ and the menu behind the cellar button

TabWhat it does
OverviewYour starting point: bottles in stock, value, what's ready to drink, your racks and search.
StockA map of each rack and wine fridge, which you swipe between. Tap a slot or a shelf to see what's in it.
＋Add something: Wine to stock, Tasted wine, Import from AI and New rack or fridge.
PromptsYour prompt library: ready-made prompts with your cellar for your own AI.
JournalAll your tastings: bottles drunk from the cellar and wines tasted elsewhere.

Tap the cellar button at the top of Overview to open the menu (More). It has three parts:

  • Library: All wines, Insights, My taste and Wish list. When you have drafts from your AI, Drafts is here too.
  • Cellars: switch between your cellars, edit them or add one with ＋ New cellar.
  • Tools & settings: Find duplicates, Backup, Import & Export, Settings and Help & introduction.

On an iPad in landscape, a sidebar with the same places replaces the tab bar, and forms use two columns. The first time you open Zélar, a short introduction covers the main ideas. You can see it again under Help & introduction.

Get started

Build your cellar in three steps

  1. Create a unit. Tap ＋ → New rack or fridge. A rack is a grid of rows and slots (A1, A2, B1 …); a wine fridge has shelves, a temperature and optional temperature zones. For boxes and loose storage there's Other. Everything can be renamed and changed later.
  2. Add your wines. Tap ＋ → Wine to stock and fill in what you know. Only the name is required. You can record producer, vintage (or NV), country, region, appellation, type, style, grapes, organic and biodynamic, alcohol, price, drinking window, a note and photos. Country, region, appellation, grapes and producer are suggested as you type.
  3. Place the bottles. Choose how many bottles and a slot in the visual slot picker, for example "Rack A · A3". Bottles without a place gather under No location, so you can sort them out later.

Already have a cellar in a spreadsheet, another app or as a pile of labels? Let your own AI do the work: see First load in the AI guide.

You can have several cellars (home, summer house, storage unit …). Switch between them in the menu under Cellars.

Everyday use

Bottles in, bottles out

Drink a bottle

Open a wine, pick a bottle and tap Drink. Add the date, a rating in half stars (½–5), the occasion, the aromas you found and a tasting note. The note belongs to that bottle, so three bottles of one wine can each have their own story. The bottle leaves your stock and goes to the Journal. Tapped too soon? Choose Undo — back in stock in the bottle's menu.

Edit stock

Bought more of a wine you already have? Edit stock on the wine's page adds bottles (with slot, purchase date and price) or removes miscounted ones. Removed bottles are simply deleted; they aren't marked as drunk.

Move bottles

Use Move on a bottle and choose the new slot in the slot picker. Under Stock you can also tap a slot and put a wine straight there.

Find a wine

Search on Overview and Stock finds wines, tastings and locations. All wines in the menu shows the full list with filters (in stock, drunk, unplaced, type, ready now and more).

Find duplicates

Did iCloud merge two devices' data, or did you import the same wine twice? Find duplicates finds matching cellars, wines and tastings and merges them once you've looked them over.

When you delete a rack

Deleting a shelf, a rack or a cellar never deletes its bottles. They just lose their place and show up under No location.

Tags

Tags, opened and needled bottles

Tags sit on bottles and tastings. Zélar has three built-in tags for bottles you've opened or poured with a needle (for example Coravin), and you can make your own under Settings → Tags.

TagUse it when …
Openedthe cork is out and the bottle stands with a stopper or vacuum stopper in.
Needled · screwcapyou poured through a screwcap, or you use a Coravin Pivot.
Needled · corkyou poured through a natural cork with a Coravin needle.

The tag remembers the date, so you can see how long the bottle has been open. With Zélar Pro you can get a reminder before it goes stale: see reminders.

The wine's profile

Aromas, serving and decanting

Besides the facts, a wine can have a profile of its own: aromas, a headline, a description, a serving temperature and a decanting time. Without a temperature, Zélar uses a default for the type (red 16, white 7, rosé 9, sparkling 7, orange 13, dessert 8, fortified 15 °C).

The aromas found in a glass belong to the bottle or the tasting; the wine's profile is the wine's own. Temperatures show in °C or °F, as you choose under Settings → Preferences → Temperature.

Drinking windows

Know when to pull the cork

Give a wine a drinking window (for example "2026–2042", optionally with a peak year), and Zélar follows where the wine is: too young, ready, at its peak, closing soon or past it. Overview shows what's ready now, and Insights also shows what to drink soon, so nothing quietly slips past its window.

Journal

Everything you've tasted

The Journal tab gathers bottles drunk from the cellar and wines tasted elsewhere. You can search wine, note and place, filter and sort.

  • Tasted a great wine out? Tap ＋ → Tasted wine and record place, date, rating, aromas, note, glass or bottle, price and photos — without it being in your cellar.
  • Bought it afterwards? Convert to stock on the tasting creates the wine with bottles. The tasting stays in the Journal, linked to the wine.
  • Tags work here too: your own tags can go on tastings.
Insights

Your cellar in numbers, and your own taste

Insights (in the menu's Library) is a dashboard of widgets you choose and arrange: value, the split by type, country, vintage and grapes, purchases and drinking over time, "Ready to drink now" and "Drink soon". Small numbers are shown honestly: one glass isn't a trend.

My taste unlocks as you rate wines. The profile is built only from your own ratings and aromas: the style you like most, your favourite grapes and your best bottles. No one else's reviews, no community score.

Wish list

Wines you want to taste

Wish list in the menu's Library is for wines you'd like to taste or buy. The list goes into your prompts, so your AI can take it into account, for example in shopping suggestions.

Use your own AI

Copy, paste, share — no API keys

Zélar works with the AI you already use: ChatGPT, Claude or another. It's free, works offline and will stay free. Zélar doesn't send anything to your AI itself; you move the text, through the clipboard, the share sheet or a Shortcut.

Prompts

Under Prompts, choose a task — food pairing for dinner, assess my cellar, shopping suggestions, the restaurant wine list, enrich my wine details or just my cellar — and tap Copy prompt. The prompt includes your cellar, your tastings and your wish list. Paste it into your AI and get answers about your own wines.

Import from AI

Has your AI read a receipt, a cellar list or a few labels for you? ＋ → Import from AI (or Prompts → Once the AI has answered) has three ways in: Paste wines from AI, Paste tasted wine from AI and Update wines from AI. Zélar reads the reply and creates the wines, with their places, or the tastings, once you've checked them. Tap ? for the format and a button to copy it to your AI. Danish and English field names both work.

Enrich your wines with your own AI

The task enrich my wine details sends your wines with a small reference mark per wine. Paste the reply back with Update wines from AI, and Zélar shows every change field by field. Nothing is overwritten until you tick it.

Share to Zélar and Shortcuts

You can share a reply straight from the Claude or ChatGPT app to Zélar, and scan a label with a Shortcut that uses your own AI. What comes in becomes a draft you check before anything is saved. It's all in the AI guide.

Export as Markdown

Backup, Import & Export → Export as Markdown turns your whole cellar into a readable document, grouped by cellar, rack and shelf, with unplaced bottles, tastings and the wish list. Put it in an AI project and your assistant always knows your collection.

Sync, backup & export

Take good care of your data

iCloud sync

Settings → iCloud sync keeps your cellar the same on your devices through your own private iCloud, photos included. The developer has no access to it. A change to the setting takes effect the next time the app starts. Your data is always on the device too, so the app works offline.

Backup, Import & Export

This screen in the menu gathers everything that goes in and out of Zélar:

  • Export database saves your whole cellar as one .zelarbackup file, with or without images, wherever you like (for example iCloud Drive or Files).
  • Restore from file replaces everything in the app with the contents of a backup file. The app asks you to confirm first.
  • Export as Markdown: see above.
  • First load: let your AI build an import from a spreadsheet, another app's export or label photos. See the AI guide.

Start over

Settings → Clear all data deletes everything on the device after two confirmations. It can't be undone, so export a backup first.

Settings

Make Zélar your own

  • Currency — the currency prices and value are shown in. Amounts are stored in the currency they were entered in and are never rewritten.
  • Temperature — °C or °F. The default follows your device.
  • Stylized rack / fridge — show your storage as furniture with bottles seen from above, or as a simple diagram.
  • Tags — your own tags for bottles and tastings.
  • Language — Zélar is available in Danish and English and follows your device's language. You can pick a language for the app alone under iOS Settings → Zélar → Language.
  • Share anonymized usage statistics — under Privacy. Anonymous statistics about which features are used help improve the app. Your wines and notes are never included, and you can turn it off at any time. See the privacy policy.

Free holds 50 bottles and 2 racks. At the limit you can still see, edit and export everything; you just can't add more until you remove some or choose Pro.

Scan label

＋ → Scan label: take a photo of the label, and Zélar AI finds the wine, its drinking window and profile. Everything can be corrected before you save, and AI can be wrong, so check it. The result offers Add to cellar or Log in journal. Until then the wine waits in Scanned wines in the menu's Library, which also keeps every scan you've made. Free gives you 5 scans in total to try it.

Enrich online

When a wine is missing details, Enrich online on the wine's page can suggest the missing facts and an estimated market value. You see the suggestions field by field, and nothing is saved until you tick them and tap Save. Only the wine's name, producer, vintage, country, region, appellation and grapes are sent.

Reminders

Under Settings → Reminders:

  • Drink reminders: at times you choose (for example Friday at 3 pm), Zélar suggests a bottle from the cellar that is past, at the end of or inside its drinking window. The same wine comes back only after the others have been suggested.
  • Suggest a dish for wines that need drinking: optional. When a wine is past its window, Zélar AI writes the reminder and suggests a dish. Only the wine's name, producer, vintage, type, origin and grapes are sent, and each wine is asked about once.
  • Opened bottles: a reminder before an opened or needled bottle goes stale. See the table below.
Tag on the bottleKeepsYou're notified
Opened1 week2 days before
Needled · screwcap (and Coravin Pivot)4 weeks4 days before
Needled · cork6 months7 days before
Needled · cork, sparkling4 weeks4 days before
Using a Coravin Pivot?

The Pivot pours from an opened bottle through a special stopper, not through the cork, so the wine keeps for less time than with a classic Coravin. Tag the bottle Needled · screwcap, so the reminder comes after 4 weeks.

The times are rules of thumb. The reminder arrives at 5 pm on the day. Notifications must be allowed for Zélar in iOS Settings.

Several currencies

With Pro you can enter amounts in currencies other than your own. Amounts keep the currency they were entered in; totals convert when they're shown, using the European Central Bank's reference rates, and converted amounts always say "approx." with the rate and date.

Your plan and fair use

With a subscription (monthly or yearly), Zélar AI is included. To keep the price down, fair use applies: up to 60 AI actions a month and 30 a day (scanning and enriching; lines in reminders don't count). If you reach the cap, Zélar AI takes a short pause; everything else in the app works as before, including Prompts with your own AI. Pro for life gives a set number of scans a month, shown on the scan and under Your plan.

Your plan is in the menu. If you bought on another device, use Restore purchases.

Share a cellar (beta)

A cellar can be shared with up to four others, each on their own Apple ID. Racks, bottles, tags and who drank what are shared; tasting notes, ratings, the Journal and the wish list stay private. The owner's Pro covers everyone in the shared cellar. Sharing is a beta you turn on under Settings → Experimental, once it's available.

Widget and app-icon shortcuts

The "Ready to drink" widget shows what's ready now on your Home Screen. Touch and hold Zélar's icon for shortcuts: Add wine, Add tasting and Scan label. The widget and the shortcuts are free.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts, sign-ups or passwords. Your data lives on your device — and in your own iCloud, if you use sync.

Can Zélar scan labels?

Yes, in two ways. For free: with a Shortcut that reads the label on your phone and asks your own Claude or ChatGPT. See the AI guide. And with Zélar AI's Scan label once Zélar Pro is released. Barcodes aren't read.

I marked a bottle as drunk by mistake.

Open the bottle's menu and choose Undo — back in stock. The bottle returns to your stock and the tasting is removed.

I have the same wine twice.

Use Find duplicates in the menu. It usually happens when iCloud merges two devices' data.

Is my data deleted if I delete the app?

Data on the device goes with the app. If iCloud sync was on, it's still in your private iCloud and comes back when you reinstall. Export a backup file before you delete, to be safe.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later.
